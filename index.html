Dohány Street Synagogue (largest in Europe), then the mural-covered lanes of the Jewish Quarter. Central Market Hall for lángos and paprika. Evening in the ruin bar district — Szimpla Kert is the original, worth one visit despite being touristy.

Trip-wide notes

Not tied to a single day — wine and food context, money, timing, and packing for the whole trip.

Wine & olive oil

Local varietalsPlavac Mali and Malvasia are the two worth asking about — Croatia exports very little of its best wine and oil, so it's genuinely a taste you won't easily find at home
Konobasmany traditional taverns produce their own wine and olive oil — worth asking the waitstaff or winemaker about flavor profile and history rather than just ordering off the list
Ujea well-regarded Croatian olive oil brand — the orange-infused version is a standout, worth bringing home

Money & pricing context

CurrencyCroatia adopted the euro in 2023 — no longer the bargain it once was
Still relatively good valuehotels, private boat charters, and guided experiences generally remain less expensive than comparable trips in France or Italy, even at the high end

Timing

Shoulder seasonApril–end of May and September–October are quieter and genuinely recommended over peak summer
Peak season realityDubrovnik, Split, and Hvar get nicknamed "the beehive" in July/August for the crowds — still a great trip, but worth knowing going in, and a reason to lean into less-touristy stops where this itinerary already does
Strategy for the coastal cities regardless of season: do city touring early morning or after 6pm, when cruise-ship crowds thin out — midday is when it's most packed.

Packing philosophy

Core ideafewer pieces, better fabrics, versatile styling — a wardrobe that moves easily between a hike, a boat, and a nicer dinner without overpacking
Fabricsbreathable, packable, wrinkle-resistant — cotton, linen, stretch knits
Sun coveragelong sleeves and airy fabrics often more comfortable than bare skin in peak sun; a hat with a tie or strap for windy boat days
Shoes, rule of threeone comfortable walking shoe for travel/touring days, one flat sandal, one dressier sandal for evenings — cobblestones and heels don't mix
Packing cubesgenuinely useful for a multi-stop trip like this one — organize by day-wear/evening-wear or by leg of the trip
Room for purchasesa foldable extra bag for the way home is worth packing, especially if buying anything fragile (coral jewelry, ceramics)
